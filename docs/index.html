Filings are compiled from public sources and shown as published. Check the official document before relying on any item. This page is for research and is not investment advice.

Sector heatmap and sector flow are worked out from the NSE Nifty 500 and F&O stock lists with daily prices and volumes from Yahoo Finance. While the market is open they include the day's session as it stands, fetched about every 12 minutes and running about 15 minutes behind the exchange; after the close they show the finished day. Commodity prices are futures prices from Yahoo Finance, fetched about every 20 minutes while the markets trade. The radar runs day and night, and an open page reloads itself when new data is published. IPO dates, lead managers, sectors and grey market premiums come from ipowatch.in and are unofficial. Data can be delayed or wrong.
